A man won one million pounds on live TV… and never got a single penny. Because of one cough.
September 2001. Major Charles Ingram, a British Army officer, sat in the hot seat on Who Wants to Be a Millionaire.
His first night was a disaster. He stumbled to four thousand pounds… and burned two of his three lifelines.
Then he came back the next day… and something changed.
He started thinking out loud. Reading every answer slowly.
And somewhere in the audience… someone coughed.
Every time he reached the right answer, a cough. When he leaned toward a wrong one… silence.
Question after question, he climbed. 32 thousand. 125 thousand. Half a million.
Then the final question. One with a hundred zeros. A googol.
He picked it. And won one million pounds.
But backstage, the sound team had heard something strange.
They counted 192 coughs during his game. 19 of them came from one man.
Tecwen Whittock. A college lecturer, sitting in the contestants' seats.
The show froze the cheque. Police were called.
In 2003, Charles, his wife Diana, and Whittock were all found guilty of deception.
Charles had to resign from the army. And the million pounds? Gone forever.
To this day, he says he's innocent. But it all came down to…
