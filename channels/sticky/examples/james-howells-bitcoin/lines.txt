Somewhere under a mountain of trash in Wales… there's a hard drive worth around 800 million dollars. And the man who owns it isn't allowed to dig it up.
His name is James Howells. An IT engineer from Newport, Wales.
Back in 2009, when almost nobody had heard of Bitcoin, he started mining it on his laptop.
In just a few weeks, he'd collected eight thousand bitcoins. Back then, they were worth almost nothing.
So he stopped mining. Saved the wallet on a small hard drive. And tossed it in a drawer.
Then in 2013, while cleaning out the house… the hard drive ended up in a bag of junk.
His girlfriend at the time drove that bag to the local dump.
A few months later, Bitcoin exploded. Those coins were suddenly worth millions.
James raced to the landfill. But the drive was already buried… under thousands of tonnes of garbage.
He begged the city council to let him search. They said no. Digging could be an environmental disaster.
So he made them an offer. A share of the fortune for the city. Then robots, AI scanners, a professional excavation team.
The answer was still no.
In 2024, he sued the council for nearly 500 million pounds.
The court threw the case out. His appeal was rejected too.
Now the landfill is set to be closed and covered over… possibly forever.
And every year Bitcoin climbs, that buried drive becomes even more valuable.
Eight thousand bitcoins. Sitting in the dark. Because…
