His climbing partner cut the rope… and he fell into the dark.
June 1985. Peru. Two British climbers, Joe Simpson and Simon Yates.
They had just conquered the west face of Siula Grande… over twenty thousand feet up.
Then, on the way down… Joe fell. His right leg broke at the knee.
Up there, that's usually a death sentence.
But Simon tied two ropes together… and lowered Joe down the mountain, three hundred feet at a time.
Then a storm hit… and Simon lowered him straight over an ice cliff.
Joe was hanging in midair… unable to climb… unable to hear.
For about an hour, Simon held on… as the snow under him gave way.
So he took out his knife… and cut the rope.
Joe fell into a deep crevasse… and landed on a ledge of ice.
The next day, Simon believed Joe was dead… and went down alone.
But Joe was alive. He lowered himself deeper… and found a way out into the sunlight.
Then he crawled… for about three days. Over five miles. Almost no food or water.
He reached camp in the middle of the night… hours before the others were leaving.
Simon woke to a weak voice in the dark… calling his name.
Joe later said Simon made the right call.
And it all started the moment…
