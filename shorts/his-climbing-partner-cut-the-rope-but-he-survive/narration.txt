His climbing partner cut the rope… and he fell into the dark.
June 1985. The Peruvian Andes. Two young British climbers, Joe Simpson and Simon Yates.
They had just climbed the west face of Siula Grande… over twenty thousand feet high.
Then, on the way down… Joe fell. And his right leg broke at the knee.
Up there, a broken leg usually means you don't come home.
But Simon didn't leave him. He tied two ropes together… and lowered Joe down the mountain, three hundred feet at a time.
Then night fell. A storm hit. And Simon lowered Joe straight over the edge of an ice cliff.
Joe was hanging in midair… unable to climb up… and unable to hear Simon.
Simon held on for about an hour… while the snow under him slowly gave way.
He had one choice left. He took out his knife… and cut the rope.
Joe dropped into a deep crevasse… and landed on a ledge of ice.
The next morning, Simon saw the crevasse… believed Joe was dead… and went down alone.
But Joe was alive. He lowered himself deeper into the crevasse… and found a way out into the sunlight.
Then he crawled… and hopped… for about three days. Over five miles of ice and rock. With almost no food or water.
Late one night, he reached the camp… just hours before the others were going to leave.
Simon heard a weak voice in the dark… calling his name.
Joe later said Simon made the right call… and they're still friends.
And it all started the moment…
