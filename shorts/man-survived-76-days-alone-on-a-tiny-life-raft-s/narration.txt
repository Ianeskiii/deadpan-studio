A man survived seventy-six days alone at sea… on a raft about six feet wide.
February 1982. Steven Callahan is sailing alone… on a tiny boat he built himself.
Seven days out from the Canary Islands… a storm hits at night.
Then something slams into the hull. Probably a whale.
Water floods in… and he scrambles onto an inflatable life raft.
By dawn, his boat is gone.
He has a spear gun, some flares… and three solar stills for drinking water.
Most days, that means about a pint of water… total.
So he hunts. Triggerfish… flying fish… barnacles… even birds.
Fish start following the raft… and he spears them to stay alive.
Then the raft starts to leak… and he spends days pumping air back in.
Nine ships pass by. He fires his flares. Not one of them sees him.
He loses about a third of his body weight… and the current keeps carrying him west.
Eighteen hundred nautical miles.
On day seventy-six, fishermen near Guadeloupe spot birds circling over something in the water.
It's Steven… still alive.
And to this day, it's hard to believe…
