How a short gets made

  1. Queue it here. It appears under Shorts as Queued.
  2. Tell Claude “run the queue” in the chat where the studio lives. Claude is the engine, so nothing renders until then.
  3. Watch it move. Stage, progress and time left update on the card while Claude works. A 20-second short takes roughly 2 to 3 hours, mostly rendering.
  4. Review and revise. Play the video, download it, or write what to change. Each revision becomes a new version.
